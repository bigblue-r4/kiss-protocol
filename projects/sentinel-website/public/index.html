Trust path

From technical enforcement to legible trust.

Sentinel starts with rigorous controls: policy enforcement, witnessing, and evidence preservation. The longer-term goal is to let that depth support simpler trust signals that buyers, operators, and eventually ordinary users can understand without reading a full incident brief.

Step 01

Enforce and observe

Build the hard layer first: explicit rules, qualified observation, incident records, and reviewable evidence.

Step 02

Assess consistently

Turn repeated review into structured trust frameworks, clearer status language, and more consistent posture decisions.

Step 03

Expose a trust signal

Eventually surface the result as a clearer verification state, certification mark, or recognizable trust label backed by the technical system beneath it.

Core capability

Firewall enforcement for agent systems.

Gatekeeper is the first product wedge: a deployable control point for inspection, policy enforcement, evidence capture, and operator review.

Layer 01 — Enforcement 🛡️

Gatekeeper Node

Gatekeeper monitors agent exchanges, evaluates them against explicit rules, pauses high-risk activity, and preserves a defensible chain of custody for review and remediation.

  • ✅ Real-time policy enforcement and execution pause
  • ✅ Signed Witness log and incident-ready evidence records
  • ✅ Operator-controlled escalation and kill-switch support

Posture outcomes

Every monitored run ends with a posture decision.

The point is not flashy scoring. The point is clear operational meaning: what happened, what rule applied, and what should happen next.

🟢
Clear
Within declared scope, provenance verified, continue operations.
🟡
Watch
Early drift detected, tighten policy, schedule follow-up review.
🟠
Contain
Material violation, channel throttled, remediation in progress.
🔴
Lockdown
Critical threat, agent quarantined, incident packet issued.
