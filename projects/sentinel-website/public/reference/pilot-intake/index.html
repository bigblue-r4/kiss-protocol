Pilot Intake

What a Sentinel engagement looks like for prospective partners and pilot teams

What Sentinel is

The Sentinel Project is a trust-and-safety infrastructure effort focused on:

  • AI-to-AI firewall controls
  • policy enforcement at the trust boundary
  • evidence-preserving monitoring
  • incident-ready reporting

Its first product wedge is Gatekeeper: a deployable control point that monitors agent interactions, applies explicit rules, and preserves reviewable records when a boundary is crossed.

Who it is for

Sentinel is aimed at teams operating in environments where trustworthy behavior, clear controls, and defensible records matter.

Typical fit includes:

  • security teams
  • enterprise AI programs
  • research labs
  • platform operators
  • policy-sensitive or higher-trust deployments

What Gatekeeper does

Gatekeeper is designed to:

  • observe agent exchanges and requested actions
  • evaluate activity against explicit rules and trust boundaries
  • allow, hold, block, or escalate based on policy
  • create qualified records for later review

Pilot engagement flow

  1. Initial intake — understand your deployment context, use case, and risk concerns
  2. Scope and trust-boundary review — identify what should be monitored, constrained, or escalated
  3. Pilot design — define the operating rules, review path, and reporting expectations
  4. Monitored evaluation — observe relevant activity and apply the agreed control posture
  5. Findings and next steps — return qualified observations, incident summaries if applicable, and recommended follow-up actions

What we ask from you

Please be prepared to provide:

  • short description of the system or workflow
  • deployment environment
  • known risks or policy concerns
  • reporting and review expectations
  • primary contact and preferred communication path

What you get back

Depending on scope, a pilot may return:

  • qualified findings
  • incident summaries
  • rule/application notes
  • evidence references
  • recommendations for next-step controls or review

Important posture notes

  • Sentinel is in active development; maturity varies by component.
  • Public claims should be interpreted in line with demonstrated capabilities and available evidence.
  • Proposed frameworks should not be mistaken for established universal standards.
  • Sentinel does not imply government affiliation, endorsement, or certification where none exists.
