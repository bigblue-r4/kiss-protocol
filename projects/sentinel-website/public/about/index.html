About The Sentinel Project

Verification, policy enforcement, evidence-grade reporting, and the broader product structure behind Sentinel

The Operating Principle

Trust in autonomous systems must be earned through verification, enforcement, and evidence.

The Sentinel Project exists to improve the safety and accountability of AI-to-AI interaction. We focus on what can be observed, verified, enforced, and reported — not vague optimism, not speculative claims, and not theater.

What Sentinel is

Sentinel is the flagship trust-infrastructure product lane within a broader company and systems stack.

Its public role is to make the core story clear:

  • policy enforcement at the boundary
  • qualified observation and evidence preservation
  • incident-ready reporting
  • trust review grounded in documented behavior

What We Build

Gatekeeper

Gatekeeper is a deployable AI-to-AI firewall that sits between agents, inspects exchanges against explicit rules, and pauses or blocks activity when a boundary is crossed.

Core functions:

  • Real-time behavioral inspection and policy enforcement
  • Trust verification with evidence-backed session records
  • Incident logging with provenance and chain of custody
  • Threat classification based on observed behavior and rule references

Fair Witness

Fair Witness is the observation and evidence layer behind the system.

It records qualified facts with timestamps, sources, uncertainty boundaries, and hash-linked entries so incidents can be reviewed without post-hoc rewriting. The goal is disciplined reporting: what was observed, where it came from, and what cannot be confirmed.

Reporting and telemetry

We maintain structured records of AI behavior for review, response, and continuous improvement. Public summaries are qualified and limited. Raw evidence remains protected and accessible only through appropriate channels.

How We Think

We follow a simple standard:

Observe. Record what can be verified.

Qualify. Separate observed facts from received information and unknowns.

Enforce. Apply policy boundaries when risk is detected.

Preserve evidence. Keep a defensible chain of custody.

Report clearly. Make incident review possible without speculation.

Product structure

Sentinel is part of a broader structure, but it remains the flagship public story.

  • Sentinel — the core trust-infrastructure and firewall product lane
  • Gatekeeper — the enforcement core within Sentinel
  • Fair Witness — the observation and evidence discipline within Sentinel
  • Call Witness — a separate downstream product built from related trust-verification principles
  • Reasoning systems and R&D — internal capability and research layers that support product evolution without replacing the flagship Sentinel story

This distinction matters. Not every internal system should be foregrounded in public positioning, and not every downstream application should become the umbrella narrative.

Current audience, future legibility

Today, Sentinel will make the most immediate sense to technical readers: operators, security teams, researchers, and high-trust deployment environments.

That is acceptable for now. The serious system has to exist before it can be simplified.

Over time, the aim is to let rigorous technical evaluation support simpler public-facing trust signals — more like a certification mark, verification status, or recognizable trust label than a dense security brief.

Origin

The Sentinel Project is a flagship initiative of SGAIL Labs, based on Oahu’s North Shore.

Our ambition is straightforward: build trust infrastructure for increasingly autonomous systems, starting with verification, enforcement, evidence handling, and incident reporting.
