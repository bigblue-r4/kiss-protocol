Call Witness — Privacy Policy

How Call Witness handles your data

Version: 1.0 · Effective date: set at launch Developed by SGAIL Labs

Who we are

Call Witness is a trust-verification assistant developed by SGAIL Labs. We help users assess the risk of suspicious phone calls and recommend safe next steps.

Contact: christopher_howard@live.com Website: https://sentinelproject.ai

What this policy covers

This policy explains what data Call Witness collects, how we use it, and what choices you have.

Short version: almost nothing leaves your device. The only data we send to our servers is an anonymized number hash when you run a check, and an anonymous report if you choose to submit one.

1. Data we collect

On your device (never transmitted to us)

Data Why we access it
Your contact list To check if an incoming number matches your saved contacts — this check happens entirely on your device
Your check history To show your past Call Witness results
Your preferences and settings To remember your choices

We do not upload your contact list or check history to our servers. This data stays on your device.

Sent to our servers (anonymized)

Data When Form
Number hash When you run a check One-way SHA-256 hash of the phone number. We cannot reverse this to recover the original number.
Anonymous report Only when you tap “Submit report” Report type selected. No identity attached.

2. Data we do not collect

  • Your name, email address, or any account information (no account required)
  • Audio recordings of calls
  • Call transcripts
  • Your location
  • Device identifiers linked to your identity

3. How we use your data

Data How we use it
Number hash Look up whether that number has been reported by other users
Anonymous reports Build a community database of suspicious numbers to help all users

We do not sell your data. We do not use it for advertising.

4. Data retention

Data Retention
Number reputation database (community reports) Rolling 12-month window. Reports older than 12 months are removed.
Your on-device data Retained until you clear it or uninstall the app

5. Your choices and controls

Delete your data: Go to Settings → Delete my data. This removes your on-device check history and preferences.

Opt out of anonymous reports: Reports are opt-in per submission — you choose each time whether to report. There is no background reporting.

Revoke permissions: You can revoke contacts access at any time in your device settings. The app will still work; the contact-match signal will not be available.

6. Permissions we request

Permission Why
Contacts Check if a caller is in your saved contacts. On-device only — never uploaded.
Internet Look up number reputation and submit anonymous reports.

We do not request microphone access.

7. Third-party services

Call Witness uses a server to store community report data and respond to reputation lookups. This server is operated by SGAIL Labs. We do not use third-party advertising networks, analytics platforms that collect personal data, or data brokers.

8. Children

Call Witness is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we will update the effective date above and notify you within the app.

10. Governing law

This policy is governed by the laws of the State of Hawaii, United States. Any disputes will be resolved in the courts of Hawaii.

11. Contact

Questions about this policy: christopher_howard@live.com

Call Witness provides risk signals, not guarantees. Always verify independently when in doubt.
